# Schedule

SICSS-Istanbul 2026 ran from **6 to 18 July 2026**, hosted across Sabancı, Piri Reis, Özyeğin, Koç and Boğaziçi universities.

Every lecture was recorded, and the slides, notebooks and datasets used in each session are shared openly. The full program can therefore be worked through independently by anyone who was not able to attend in person.

📁 **[All course materials (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_JicgdomYEGcOkSSIRVE1mA0sETE6tag)**
▶️ **[All lecture recordings (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAiowMTy1GXxVuiSPBYuN4re8TdD9gKsY)**
