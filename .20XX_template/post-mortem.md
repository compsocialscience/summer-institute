<h2 class="display-4">Summer Institutes in Computational Social Science [[YEAR]] Post-mortem</h2>

We've just completed the [[YEAR]] [Summer Institutes in Computational Social Science](https://sicss.io/). The purpose of the Summer Institutes is to bring together graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and beginning faculty interested in computational social science. The Summer Institutes are for both social scientists (broadly conceived) and data scientists (broadly conceived). This summer we had a mixture of in-person institutes and virtual institutes across the world. In addition to various partner locations run by SICSS alumni, we continue to grow our network of computational social science researchers.

These post-mortems describe, for each site, a) how the Institute was run, b) what each site thinks worked well, and c) what each site will do differently next time. We hope that this will be useful to others organizing similar Summer Institutes or future organizers of SICSS sites. If you are interested in hosting a partner location of SICSS at your university, company, NGO, or governmental organization, please read our information for [potential partner locations](https://compsocialscience.github.io/summer-institute/host).

This page includes post-mortem reports from all locations in order to facilitate comparisons, as well as an overview of key themes and takeaways. As you will see, different sites did things differently, and we think that this kind of customization was an important part of how we were successful.

For guide & instructions on adding post-mortems, see: https://github.com/compsocialscience/summer-institute/blob/master/documentation/adding-post-mortems.md
